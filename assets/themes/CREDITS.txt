NIXIE GLASS HOUSING
nixie-glass-shell.png is an AI-edited transparent derivative of
Dalibor Farny RZ568M Nixie Tube.jpg by TofDir, CC0 1.0:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dalibor_Farny_RZ568M_Nixie_Tube.jpg
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Edited for Tokumaru Labs using OpenAI imagegen, October 2026. The room background,
lit digit, mesh and internal supports were removed; the glass housing, reflections
and black socket were retained as a separate overlay. CSS adjusts its proportions,
opacity and clipping. Source URL, method and prompt are recorded in
nixie-glass-shell-provenance.json. This is an edited illustration assembled with
the Z566M digit photographs credited below, not an exact reproduction or unmodified
photograph of either tube model. No dimming or idle brightness behavior is used.
